Sharing and connections

Who can see what I log?
Only you, until you invite someone. When you do, they receive a summary our server builds from your settings — not your raw log. Your private notes and the sensitive parts of your Care Profile can never be shared at all. The Privacy Policy lists exactly which fields can travel and which cannot.
How do I connect someone?
Go to Connections, create an invitation, and send them the code. They download Men's True Aide, enter the code, and complete a short setup. The code works once and expires after 14 days.
How do I stop sharing?
Settings → Data & Sharing → remove the connection, or do it from the Connections screen. It takes effect immediately: the summaries and suggested actions built for that person are deleted and their app goes blank. You don't have to explain, and there is no message for you to write.
Can I share some things and not others?
Yes. Each connection has its own settings, so you can share more with one person than another. Your Care Profile is off unless you turn it on.
My code says invalid or expired.
Codes expire after 14 days and can only be used once. Create a fresh one. Codes are not case-sensitive, but a stray space pasted from a message will stop it working — try typing it by hand.

Your cycle

My day count is stuck, or the phase looks wrong.
The app works from the period start dates you've recorded. If you haven't recorded a new one in a while, it will keep counting from the last one it knows about. Tap Did your period start? on Home, or use the setup card, to record a start date — everything recalculates from there.
I skipped the start date when I signed up.
That's fine, it's an allowed answer. Home will show a card inviting you to add one whenever you're ready. Until then the app won't guess, and nothing about your cycle is sent to a supporter.
I recorded the wrong date.
Record the correct one — the most recent start date is what the app uses. If you need history corrected, email us.
How accurate are the predictions?
They're estimates from your own dates and they're often off. Cycles move for all sorts of reasons. See the Health Notice.

Logging

Can I log for a past day?
Yes — pick the date at the top of the Log screen. Editing a past day updates anything built from it.
The microphone isn't there.
Dictation needs permission for speech recognition and the microphone. Check Settings → Hey Hun on your phone. Dictation runs entirely on your device — no audio is recorded, uploaded, or kept.
Can I hide sections I never use?
Yes. Settings → Log Preferences lets you hide and reorder sections. Feelings stays pinned.
What's the difference between a private note and a shareable note?
The private note is stored apart from everything else and no sharing feature can reach it. The shareable note is the one that can go to a supporter if you've turned that on.

Notifications

I'm not getting notifications.
Check Settings → Hey Hun → Notifications on your phone. We only notify you about your connections — when someone accepts your invitation and when they finish setting up — plus the weekly recap if you turned it on. We never notify you about anything you logged.
Can I turn the weekly recap off?
Yes, it's off unless you switch it on, and you can switch it back off in Settings at any time.

Account

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete Account, then type the confirmation. It removes your profile, logs, cycle history, private notes, Care Profile, and every connection with its summaries. It cannot be undone. If you can't get into the app, email us from your account's address and we'll do it for you.
I signed in with Apple and Google and now I have two accounts.
Accounts link only when the email addresses match. Apple's Hide My Email creates a different address, so it makes a separate account. Email us and we'll sort it out.
Why do you need my date of birth?
Only to confirm you're 18 or over. It's asked once, can't be edited afterwards, and is never shared with anyone you connect to.
