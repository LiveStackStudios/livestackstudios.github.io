Once connected, a supporter receives a filtered summary built by our server according to your current settings — never your raw log, and never anything you have marked private. Your supporter can also end the connection from their side.

Please connect only people you actually trust. We can control what leaves your account; we cannot control what a person does with what they see, or take a screenshot of.
