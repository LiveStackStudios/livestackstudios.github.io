Hey Hun Health Notice

Last updated 17 August 2026

Hey Hun is a journal, not a medical device. It is not a doctor, not a diagnosis, and not birth control. Nothing it shows you should replace advice from a qualified clinician.

What the app actually does

Hey Hun records what you tell it and does arithmetic on the dates. When it says you are on a particular day of your cycle, or names a phase, or shows a predicted next period, that is a calculation from the period start dates you entered and an estimate of your typical cycle length — nothing more. It does not measure anything about your body.

Predictions are estimates and they are frequently wrong. Cycles shift with stress, illness, travel, sleep, exercise, medication, breastfeeding, perimenopause, and for no discernible reason at all. If the app has few dates to work from, or your cycles are irregular, the estimate is weaker still. When we are not confident, we say so rather than showing you a number that looks certain.

Not contraception, not fertility guidance

Hey Hun must not be used to prevent or achieve pregnancy. It is not a fertility awareness method: it does not track basal body temperature, cervical fluid, or hormone tests, and it applies none of the clinical rulesets that certified methods require. If you are trying to conceive or trying not to, speak to a healthcare provider about a method designed for it.

Not a diagnosis

Symptoms, moods, and pain that you log are your own notes. The app does not interpret them clinically and cannot detect or rule out any condition — including PCOS, endometriosis, thyroid conditions, fibroids, infection, or pregnancy. Patterns you notice in your own log may be worth raising with a clinician; they are a starting point for a conversation, not an answer.

Please see a clinician if

  • Your bleeding is unusually heavy, or lasts unusually long.
  • You bleed between periods, after sex, or after menopause.
  • Pain is severe, or is stopping you from doing ordinary things.
  • Your periods stop, or change pattern markedly, without an explanation you are confident in.
  • You have a fever, faintness, or symptoms that feel wrong to you.
  • You are, or might be, pregnant.

Do not delay getting care because an app looked reassuring. It does not know.

If you are in crisis. In the United States, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For domestic violence, call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788. In an emergency, call 911. Outside the US, contact your local emergency number. Hey Hun is not monitored, and nobody reads your logs — writing something in the app does not reach a human being.

What your supporter sees

If you connect a supporter, they receive a summary based on the same estimates, in the same app that suggests everyday things they might do — bring you something, take a chore off you, leave you alone for a while. Those suggestions are ordinary kindness, not treatment. Neither app gives medical advice to either of you, and no suggestion should be read as one.

Your supporter is not a clinician either. The app is designed to help someone be thoughtful, not to make them responsible for your health.

Allergies and dietary limits

If you enter allergies, dietary limits, or medications in your Care Profile, our server filters suggested actions so that a suggestion naming something you listed is not sent. We take this seriously and we test it. Even so, do not rely on it as a safety control — treat every food, drink, or medicine as something to check yourself, exactly as you would if a friend brought it to you unprompted. Software fails; your own check is the one that counts.

Regulatory position

Hey Hun is a general wellness and journaling app. It is not a regulated medical device and has not been evaluated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration or any equivalent authority. LiveStack Studios is not a healthcare provider, and using this app does not create a clinician–patient relationship. We are not a HIPAA covered entity and the information you enter here is not protected health information under HIPAA — it is protected by the privacy commitments in this app and by applicable consumer privacy and health-data laws.
