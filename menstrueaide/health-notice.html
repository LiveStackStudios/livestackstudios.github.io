Men's True Aide Health Notice

Last updated 17 August 2026

This app is not medical anything. Not a diagnosis, not a device, not birth control, not a fertility method. It shows you an estimate built from dates another person entered, and suggests ordinary, everyday ways to be useful.

The cycle information is an estimate

When the app names a phase, a day number, or a predicted date, that is arithmetic on the period start dates she recorded and an estimate of her typical cycle length. Nothing about her body is being measured. Cycles shift with stress, illness, travel, sleep, exercise, medication, and for no visible reason at all — so the estimate is often wrong, sometimes by a lot. When our confidence is low, the app says so instead of showing you a number that looks certain.

Do not use this app to keep track of whether she might be pregnant, or to plan around fertility. It is not a fertility awareness method, it measures no fertility signals, and it applies none of the rules a real method requires. Using it that way is unsafe and is a breach of the Terms of Use.

Missions are kindness, not treatment

A mission is a suggestion — bring something, take a chore off her, give her some quiet. It is not care, not therapy, and not a treatment plan. Doing it does not fix anything medical, and skipping it does not harm her health. You are not her clinician and this app does not make you one.

Do not diagnose her

Symptoms and moods you see are her own notes on her own day, not clinical findings. The app cannot detect or rule out any condition, and neither can you from a summary card. If something in what she shares worries you, the useful move is to ask her how she is and, if it seems right, encourage her to see a clinician — not to tell her what she has, and not to explain her own body to her.

Situations that need a professional, not an app

Encourage her to seek care if she has unusually heavy or long bleeding, bleeding between periods or after menopause, severe pain, periods that stop or change pattern without explanation, fever or faintness, or if she is or might be pregnant. In an emergency, call your local emergency number — in the US, 911.

If either of you is in crisis: in the US, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Nobody at LiveStack Studios monitors this app, and nothing in it reaches a human being.

Allergies and food

Our server filters out any suggestion that names something she has listed as an allergy or dietary limit, and we test that filter. Even so, you are the check that counts. Before you hand her food, a drink, or anything medicinal, verify it yourself the way you would if you had thought of it on your own. Software fails; the label in your hand does not.

The app's tone

The military framing is a joke aimed at you, to make a daily habit stick. It is not aimed at her, her cycle is not a threat, and none of this is a campaign against a person. If the tone ever leads you to treat her as a problem to be managed, close the app — it is doing the opposite of its job.

Regulatory position

Men's True Aide is a general wellness app. It is not a regulated medical device and has not been evaluated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration or any equivalent authority. LiveStack Studios is not a healthcare provider, and using this app does not create a clinician–patient relationship. We are not a HIPAA covered entity, and the information shown here is not protected health information under HIPAA — it is protected by the commitments in our Privacy Policy, by her settings, and by applicable consumer privacy and health-data laws.
