Men's True Aide Support

Show up on the right day, with the right thing. Here's how to get help.

Getting connected

How do I get started?
She creates an invitation in Hey Hun and sends you the code. You enter it on the pairing screen, complete Basic Training — five short screens — and the briefing turns on. Without a code from her there is nothing to connect to.
My code says invalid, expired, or already used.
Codes work once and expire after 14 days. Ask for a fresh one. A stray space pasted from a message will also break it, so try typing the six characters by hand. You cannot redeem a code you created, and you cannot redeem a second code from the same person.
Can I try the app before I have a code?
Yes — tap "Explore the app" on the pairing screen. It runs a fictional example entirely on your phone, writes nothing to our servers, and exits in one tap.
Can I support more than one person?
Yes. Each one is a separate target with its own briefing, missions, and streak. Add another from Settings → Targets, and tap a target card to switch to it.

The briefing

It says STANDING BY and there's no briefing.
That means there's nothing to report yet — usually because she hasn't logged, or hasn't recorded a period start date so the app can't work out where she is. It is not a bug and it is not something to chase her about. It fills in when she logs.
Some sections are empty or missing.
She decides what's shared, per person, and can change it whenever she likes. If something is not there, she has not turned it on. Please don't ask her to.
The briefing looks wrong for what's actually going on.
The cycle information is an estimate from her recorded dates and it's often off. See the Health Notice. Trust her over the app, always.
Everything vanished.
The connection ended — either she revoked it or it was removed from your side. Access stops immediately and the summaries are deleted. She doesn't owe you an explanation, and the app won't tell you why.

Missions, streaks and badges

I got the same mission repeatedly.
Fixed. The app now remembers recent missions and rotates through the pool before repeating one. The exception is deliberate: if she asks for the same specific thing every day, you'll get that mission every day — her actual request always beats variety.
Does she see my score?
No. She never sees a score, a broken streak, or a badge you haven't earned. There is no scoreboard she can catch you failing on.
My streak reset.
Then it reset. One check-in starts the next run. Nothing else happens, and nobody is told.
The mission isn't appropriate today.
Skip it. It's a suggestion, not an order — you know your situation and the app doesn't.

Notifications, sound, settings

I'm not getting the briefing notification.
Check Settings → Men's True Aide → Notifications on your phone. We ask permission once, after Basic Training. If you declined, the app still works — you just open it yourself.
Can I hide notification text from my lock screen?
Yes, and it's worth doing if other people can see your phone. Settings → Notifications → Men's True Aide → Show Previews → When Unlocked.
Background audio won't play, or plays when I don't want it.
It's off by default; there's a global toggle in Settings and one per target. It respects your phone's silent switch and never records anything — the app has no microphone permission at all.

Account

How do I leave a connection?
Settings → Targets → the target → Remove. Your access ends immediately and its summaries and missions are deleted.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete Account, then type the confirmation. It ends every connection you hold, deletes their summaries, missions, and your activity records, and removes your sign-in. It cannot be undone. If you can't get into the app, email us from your account's address.
Can you tell me what she's sharing, or turn something on?
No. Those are her settings and only she can change them. We won't act on her data at your request.
One rule above all the others. What you see here is hers. Don't screenshot it, don't forward it, don't bring it up as leverage. If you're using this app to keep tabs on someone rather than to look after them, delete it.
